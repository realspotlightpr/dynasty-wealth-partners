Meet our Founder + CEO

Gary Kauffman

Gary is the founder of Dynasty Wealth Partners, a firm dedicated to helping physicians and surgeons navigate the complexities of financial planning. His passion for starting his business is driven from watching his step father's family business grow as a child and seeing the impact that it could have on their family, clients, and community.

With a deep understanding of the unique challenges faced by medical professionals, Gary specializes in strategic wealth management, tax efficiency, and retirement planning. With years of experience and a passion for empowering others, Gary is committed to guiding doctors through every phase of their financial journey — whether they are in training, aspiring affluents, or achieving financial independence. Gary has been recognized by Forbes and MDRT as a top 100 financial planning professional in the country.

Outside of work, Gary enjoys playing tennis and golf with clients and friends, watching the UFC and College Football, and long walks through the park with his wife, Julia, and golden retriever, Kipper.

01Who do you work with?+

We specialize in physicians and surgeons at every stage of their financial journey — from Doctors in Training just beginning their careers, to Aspiring Affluents building their financial futures, to Financially Independent Physicians enjoying the rewards of their hard work.

02What happens on the first call?+

The first conversation is a Discovery Meeting: we get to know you and your money story, ask about what keeps you up at night, and meet you exactly where you are in your career — whether you're in residency handling student loans or a practice owner getting ready to exit. No pressure, no obligation.

03Can you help with my student loans?+

Yes. We analyze your financial situation and loan terms to create a customized repayment strategy that minimizes interest, maximizes savings, and aligns with your long-term financial goals.

04How often will we meet once I'm a client?+

At least four times each year. Your life changes, tax laws change — so your plan should change too. These regular checkups make sure your plan always fits your current life.
